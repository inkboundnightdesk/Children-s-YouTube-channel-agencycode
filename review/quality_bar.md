# Quality Bar

Compliance says what we may not ship. **This says whether it is worth shipping.**

The two are connected: YouTube demotes "low-quality mass-produced content with little effort or
originality" under its quality principles for kids content, so the quality bar *is* part of the compliance
posture. A channel that only clears the legal minimum gets buried anyway.

## 1. Does it have a reason to exist?

- [ ] It **teaches**, **soothes**, or **delights**. Name which: ______________________
- [ ] A parent could say what their child got from it.
- [ ] It is **not** just "a rhyme with pictures over it, to fill a slot."

> If you cannot answer this one, stop. Nothing below fixes it.

## 2. Is it genuinely distinct?

- [ ] Different rhyme, setting, characters **and** structure from recent uploads — not a reskin.
- [ ] Duplicate check is PASS, or FLAG with a written reason it is genuinely different.
- [ ] It would be recognisable as its own video in a list of our thumbnails.
- [ ] **It is not the same video with the animal swapped.** That is the pattern NN-3 exists to stop.

## 3. Craft

- [ ] Animation is smooth. No jitter, no popping, no boiling backgrounds.
- [ ] Character design is consistent across every scene.
- [ ] Colour palette is coherent and matches the brief.
- [ ] Composition is uncluttered — one clear subject per frame.
- [ ] Transitions are gentle and motivated.
- [ ] Text overlays are legible at phone size and on screen long enough to read twice.

## 4. Audio

- [ ] Vocals are clear and in tune.
- [ ] Words are intelligible to a three-year-old — **listen at low volume on a phone speaker**, which is
      how most of the audience will actually hear it.
- [ ] Music and voice are balanced; nothing buried.
- [ ] Levels are consistent throughout. No sudden jumps.
- [ ] Tempo suits the audience: unhurried, singable, easy to join.

## 5. Pacing

- [ ] Opens within 3 seconds — no long logo sting, no dead air.
- [ ] Shots are held long enough to take in.
- [ ] Runtime is 90–150s, and every second earns its place.
- [ ] **No padding.** Filler to hit a runtime target is worse than a shorter video.
- [ ] Ends cleanly and calmly.

## 6. The three real tests

- [ ] **The mute test** — watch with the sound off. Does it still make sense?
- [ ] **The phone test** — watch on a phone at arm's length. Is the thumbnail text legible? The overlays?
- [ ] **The loop test** — watch it twice in a row. Does it become irritating? This audience *will* loop it,
      and the parent in the room is the one who decides whether your channel gets played again.

- [ ] **REVISE** — fixes listed below
- [ ] **REJECT** — not worth finishing
